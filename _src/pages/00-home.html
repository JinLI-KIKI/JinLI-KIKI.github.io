University of Tokyo and Dalhousie University, 2022 – now

Using 280 nm light to clean drinking water

UV-LEDs split hydrogen peroxide or chlorine into radicals that break down what normal treatment misses. I measure how much of that chemistry survives in real water, and what by-products it leaves.

{{spectrum:mini}}

  • Reactive species

    How pH, temperature and the water matrix change the radicals UV-LED/H2O2 and UV-LED/chlorine produce.

    In progressPhD research

  • Taste and odour in cold water

    Removing geosmin and 2-MIB, the compounds behind earthy, musty tap water, when ozone struggles in winter.

    PublishedDWT, 2026

  • Real water at the dosing point

    Coagulated water from a full-scale plant: organic matter and disinfection by-product potential after treatment.

    In progressPhD chapter

  • Partner projects in Canada

    UV-LED and chlorine disinfection for Halifax Water, and a flow-through UV-LED reactor for AquiSense.
