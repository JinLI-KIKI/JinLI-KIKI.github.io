Supply chain digital twin

A model of a tariff-driven factory transfer for an anonymous laptop maker, on synthetic data. Each module answers a planning question I ran into in practice. The Python engines are mirrored in JavaScript, so the report pages recompute live in the browser, and tests keep the two engines in exact agreement.

Practical rules come from my own planning experience, generalised and anonymised. Freight rates, duties and ramp times come from public sources, cited in the repository.

Built with AI assistance

I conceived and designed this project, and I make the decisions on every part of it. Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic, helps with the build under my direction.

My part
The overall concept and model design, the question each module answers, the business rules from my planning experience, judging whether every result holds up, reviewing and editing the code, and deciding what goes in.
AI assistant (Claude)
Writing and testing code to my specification, collecting public data with its sources, and drafting documentation and report pages for my review.

Every rule and number in the repository is labelled as practitioner input, public source or placeholder, so you can see what came from where.

UV-LED usage logger

2026, Oguma Lab

My supervisor wanted to know who used each UV-LED setup, for how long, and when its settings changed, because irradiance data was hard to trust without that record. I built a small web app opened from QR codes at each setup. Lab members log a session with a start and stop button, or log a calibration change: distance, measured irradiance, meter and wavelength. Records go to a shared Google Sheet through Google Apps Script.

It's now how the lab keeps track of its UV-LED equipment.

UV-AOP literature map

2026, in progress

A Python pipeline over Scopus records that sorts papers on UV advanced oxidation by light source (UV-LED, mercury lamp, excimer), filters them by research facets such as real water or radical quantification, and plots publication trends and the gaps. It checks its own tagging against a blind-scored sample. It supports the literature review in my thesis and a planned review article.
