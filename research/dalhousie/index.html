MASc research, Dalhousie University

Partner projects in Canada

My master's at Dalhousie's Centre for Water Resources Studies was built around two industry partners: Halifax Water, the region's water utility, and AquiSense, a UV-LED reactor maker.

When
Sep 2022 – May 2024
Supervisor
Prof. Graham Gagnon
Partners
Halifax Water (Sep 2022 – Jul 2023), AquiSense Technologies (Sep 2022 – May 2024)
Degree
GPA 4.07out of 4.30, first in the programme, full scholarship

How I worked

Each partner brought a question. I turned it into an experimental plan, ran the work, analysed it in R and Python and reported back with recommendations. I finished the planned experimental programme ahead of schedule.

Halifax Water

Sep 2022 – Jul 2023

UV-LED and chlorine disinfection

Published in AWWA Water Science, 2025, first author.

Could 280 nm UV-LEDs strengthen chlorine disinfection without adding by-products? I measured inactivation of T1 bacteriophage by UV-LED light alone and in sequence with chlorine, in both orders, and the formation of THMs and HAAs in filtered water exposed to 20, 40 and 80 mJ/cm².

Both sequential treatments improved disinfection, and the UV fluences used did not significantly change THM or HAA formation.

Sep 2022 – Mar 2024

UV-LED and ozone oxidation of drinking water

Compared ozone, UV/H2O2 and UV/Cl2, and related changes in natural organic matter to disinfection by-product formation potential. This work fed into my master's thesis.

2022 – 2023

Medium-pressure UV and UV-LED compared

For the utility's technology evaluation, I compared medium-pressure mercury UV with UV-LED alternatives: how each works, how it performs and what it means to operate.

Mar – Aug 2023

By-products in the distribution system

Tracked how disinfection by-products change with time in the pipes, tested the effect of adding chlorine partway through the system, and modelled chlorine residual with THM and HAA formation potential across distribution scenarios.

AquiSense

Sep 2023 – Apr 2024

A flow-through UV-LED reactor

Assembled, configured and characterised an AquiSense PearlAqua Thinfilm 534A2, a bench-scale flow-through UV-LED reactor. I tested how well it degraded different chemicals under different conditions and gave the manufacturer recommendations on design and operation, which made the unit easier to operate and adapt.

Also

Sep 2023 – Mar 2024

Ultra-pure water for hydrogen

Compared combinations of UV, ozone, H2O2 and membranes for removing organic carbon and metals from water meant for electrolysis-based hydrogen production.

2025

Photolysis with high-intensity UV-C LEDs

Co-author on a study of chemical-free degradation of trace organic contaminants by bespoke photolysis with high-intensity UV-C LEDs, published in ACS ES&T Water.

2022 – 2024

Teaching and service

Teaching assistant for MINE 4300. Co-chair of the master's student group at the Centre for Water Resources Studies.
